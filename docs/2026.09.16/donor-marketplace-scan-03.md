# GMKtec K12 — Ryzen 7 H 255 / 16 GB / 512 GB — Rp10,000,000

## Why the price matters

The K12 is not an old low-end donor. GMKtec currently lists the K12 with:

- Ryzen 7 H 255, Zen 4, 8C/16T;
- Radeon 780M, 12 CU;
- dual DDR5-5600 SO-DIMM;
- three M.2 2280 PCIe 4.0 slots;
- native OCuLink PCIe 4.0 ×4;
- USB4;
- dual 2.5 GbE;
- 19 V / 6.32 A, 120 W adapter;
- selectable roughly 45 W / 54 W / 65–70 W performance modes;
- vapor-chamber / dual-fan cooling.

Official source:
- https://www.gmktec.com/products/gmktec-k12-amd-ryzen%E2%84%A2-7-h-255-mini-pc

Current Indonesian references found during this scan:

- GMKtec Indonesia K12 listings show configured systems around **Rp15.999m+** depending memory/storage variant.
- Another Indonesian seller exposes a K12 listing range around **Rp13.7125m–14.9625m**, including a 16 GB / 512 GB variant.

Sources:
- https://shopee.co.id/MINI-PC-Gaming-AMD-Ryzen-7-H-255-GMKTEC-K12-32GB-RAM-DDR5-1TB-SSD-NVME-Win-11-pro-4K-Video-i.1415849256.27572626201
- https://shopee.co.id/Mini-PC-Gaming-AMD-Ryzen-7-H-255-GMKTEC-K12-32GB-RAM-DDR5-1TB-SSD-NVME-Win-11-pro-4K-Video-i.52887172.57958146996

At **Rp10m**, this used-under-one-week unit is therefore materially below current local new asking prices, even though it only has 16 GB / 512 GB.

## X220 Neo interpretation

This is the first marketplace listing in this scan that can realistically challenge the K8 Plus as the **main donor**, rather than merely being an experimental side branch.

Advantages:

- modern Zen 4 CPU;
- Radeon 780M;
- native OCuLink;
- USB4;
- DDR5 SO-DIMM;
- 19 V DC input;
- three NVMe slots;
- full modern connectivity;
- enough compute headroom to justify mechanical fabrication.

Penalties:

- full K12 chassis is larger than K8 Plus; naked PCB dimensions still require physical measurement;
- 65–70 W mode is too aggressive for an X220-style thermal envelope unless heavily redesigned;
- donor display remains external-output based, so X220 panel still needs display conversion;
- battery and keyboard integrations remain custom.

## Buy target

For this exact 16 GB / 512 GB almost-new unit:

- **≤ Rp9.0m:** excellent donor buy;
- **Rp9.0m–10.0m:** strong / justified if condition and warranty check out;
- **Rp10.0m–10.5m:** still reasonable because of current local new pricing;
- **> Rp11m:** compare directly against K8 Plus and newer boxed units.

### Checks before purchase

Require:

1. invoice / purchase date;
2. serial number and warranty status;
3. BIOS photo confirming Ryzen 7 H 255;
4. CPU-Z / HWiNFO screenshots;
5. CrystalDiskInfo for the 512 GB SSD;
6. RAM module layout and brand;
7. proof that OCuLink and USB4 ports work;
8. stress test / temperatures in Balanced mode;
9. original 120 W adapter.

**Decision: strongest deal of these three.**

The used Facebook unit has twice the RAM and about twice the storage of the common 16/512 configuration, but the CPU is still a 2019-era 3500U. Those RAM/SSD upgrades do not make the processor or graphics platform younger.

## Buy target

For a used 32 GB / 1 TB G10:

- **≤ Rp4.5m:** attractive;
- **Rp4.5m–5.0m:** fair if SSD/RAM are good brands and health is excellent;
- **Rp5.0m–5.5m:** marginal;
- **Rp6.5m:** too high for X220 Neo donor use.

At Rp6.5m the buyer is paying for capacity rather than platform quality. For X220 Neo, modern CPU/GPU/expansion matters more than getting 32 GB + 1 TB preinstalled.

**Decision: negotiate or skip.**

## Price interpretation

At Rp5.999m this Facebook listing is **not obviously overpriced relative to Indonesian retail**. As a normal complete low-power PC it is defensible.

For X220 Neo, however, the N150 is soldered and the platform lacks the performance and expansion ceiling of the K12/K8 Plus class.

## Buy target for donor use

- **≤ Rp4.0m:** interesting low-power donor;
- **Rp4.0m–4.5m:** acceptable if the project intentionally targets low-power operation;
- **Rp4.5m–5.0m:** weak;
- **Rp5.999m:** fair as a mini-PC, poor as a sacrificial X220 Neo donor.

**Decision: only buy if the goal changes to an ultra-low-power X220 Neo.**

# Value ranking for these three

For X220 Neo at the exact asking prices shown:

1. **GMKtec K12 at Rp10m** — strongest value and strongest donor candidate.
2. **GMKtec G3 Plus at Rp5.999m** — price is defensible, but performance ceiling is too low for the main build.
3. **GMKtec G10 at Rp6.5m** — poor value relative to both its age and current new 16/512 local pricing.

The K12 is the only one here that deserves active seller verification rather than passive watchlisting.
