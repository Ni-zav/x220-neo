# ThinkPad X220 Baseline

## Verified external envelope

Lenovo-era product specifications list the X220 at approximately:

- **305.0 mm × 206.5 mm** footprint;
- **19–34.6 mm** thickness depending on location/configuration;
- around **1.3 kg** starting weight.

These are overall-machine numbers, **not internal clearances**.

## Original platform features relevant to the transplant

- 12.5-inch display class.
- Classic 7-row keyboard and TrackPoint.
- Original keyboard is a matrix keyboard plus separate TrackPoint signalling; community reverse engineering documents the connector and matrix.
- Original X220 batteries include:
  - 44: 14.4 V nominal, 44 Wh;
  - 44+: 11.1 V nominal, 63 Wh;
  - 44++: 11.1 V nominal, 94 Wh.

## What we reuse

**Target reuse:**
- lid and rear cover;
- hinges if structurally practical;
- keyboard;
- TrackPoint/buttons;
- palmrest / keyboard bezel;
- optionally speakers and ThinkLight hardware.

**Not assumed reusable without redesign:**
- motherboard;
- original LCD signalling/cable;
- battery charge path;
- original DC power path;
- original fan/heatsink.

## Strong recommendation

Buy a **spare X220 bottom case** before cutting the original. Current Indonesian listings found during research were around Rp105k–125k for lower shells, while palmrest/bezel pieces appeared around Rp149k. See the price snapshot for links.
