# Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Naked K8 board/cooler does not fit expected Z-height | Medium | High | Do not finalize CAD before physical measurement; allow thicker P0 shell |
| Fan recirculates hot exhaust | Medium | High | Dedicated duct and closed-shell thermal testing |
| Universal LCD controller incompatible with exact panel | Medium | Medium | Bench test before mounting; buy from seller with exact resolution/panel support |
| Keyboard DIY consumes too much time | Medium | Medium | TP Art controller is fallback |
| USB4 PD role misunderstood | Medium | High | Use verified 19 V DC jack for V0 |
| Original X220 battery reuse becomes a rabbit hole | High | High | Defer battery until stable V1 |
| Port-extension signal integrity issues | Medium | Medium | Keep high-speed USB4/OCuLink paths short; expose donor connectors directly where possible |
| Hinge loads crack printed shell | Medium | High | Preserve/reinforce metal structure; test with spare shell |
| Marketplace RAM price/variant mismatch | High | Medium | Verify variant at checkout; prefer reputable seller and memtest |
| eGPU hot-plug damages/locks system | Medium | High | Power off before OCuLink connect/disconnect |
| Cutting debris shorts electronics | Medium | High | Electronics removed during cutting; full clean before reassembly |
