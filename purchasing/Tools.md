## Recommended, not day-one essential

- ESD mat + wrist strap;
- helping-hands PCB holder;
- USB power meter;
- bench DC supply with current limit;
- hot-air station for SMD work;
- inexpensive IR thermometer or thermocouple probe;
- metal ruler + machinist square;
- deburring tool.

## Rough starter-bench budget

A workable starter setup is roughly **Rp1.3m–1.8m**, depending on how much is already owned and whether the soldering station/rotary tool are bought immediately.

## No, you do not really need a wrench 😭

The project is mostly precision Phillips/Torx/hex bits, soldering, measuring, filing and rotary-tool work. A small needle-nose plier is more useful than a conventional wrench.
